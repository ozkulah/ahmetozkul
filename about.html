about

The short name, and the longer one

I am an ML engineer. Most of my work is edge AI: models that have to run on a device, under power and latency limits, often without a reliable network. I live in Stockholm. I hike, and I help organize walks with Sthlm Hikers.

travellerip is the short form of a name I already use. The longer form is traveller in prison. It comes from my own life: injustice, time in prison, and leaving the country I was in. I keep the details for when I choose to write them. This page is only the origin of the handle.

This is a personal nickname. It is not about the Irish Traveller community or the Travellers in Prison Initiative in Ireland.

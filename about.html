About

I am a computer engineer in Stockholm. I started with robots — both of my degrees at Istanbul Technical University ended in humanoid-robotics theses — and then spent years on the unglamorous half of ML: train it properly, then make it small enough to live on the device.

Since 2021 that work has been edge AI and TinyML at Imagimob. Before that I built data and software systems as a freelancer, at Roksit, at Siemens, and for several years at TÜBİTAK.
